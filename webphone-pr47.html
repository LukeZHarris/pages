Summary

Replaces an eager-and-manual ICE restart with a SIP.js-driven re-INVITE, debounced on the transient disconnected state. Adds lifecycle cleanup and tightens success/failure reporting. Also raises iceGatheringTimeout from 500 ms to 3000 ms.

Verdict: Approve with nits. The core fix (let SIP.js own the offer/answer for ICE restart) is the right call — it removes a documented race that caused silent first call after restart. Lifecycle handling is solid. A few small UX/robustness items below.
